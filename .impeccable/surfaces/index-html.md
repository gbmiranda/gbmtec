# Surface brief: index.html (gbmtec.com.br)

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine; the ambition lives in the contract below).

Audience and job: founders and product leads in Brazil, plus tech leads and recruiters who open the LinkedIn. In seconds they must know who Gabriel is, see what he already shipped, and reach him. Action: write to him with the project (form that composes an e-mail), second action: open a live product.
Proof and content: four live products (Gabaritei, UpUai, Petalia, Estilia) as real screenshots, LinkedIn career 2012 to today, the one sourced number (Araujo app, R$ 20 mi/mês), company data (razão social, CNPJ).
Constraints: pt-BR, no em dash as punctuation, static HTML/CSS/JS, hosted on UpUai (project diversos), light and dark, mobile first, company data and Organization JSON-LD kept.
Memorable moment: the Didone monogram is drawn once by a pen line and then inked, then the first viewport shows the work itself through four square windows cut into the page.
Unresolved: WhatsApp number, prices, testimonials (none confirmed, none invented).

## Direction contract

THESIS: a showroom of strict square lattices where the only color in the room is the work Gabriel shipped. Refuses the dark neon developer portfolio and the white SaaS agency template with a headline over a row of cards.
OWN-WORLD: gallery white ground ruled in lacquer black, hammered silver for hairlines and idle frames, letterspaced geometric capitals (Josefin Sans light) for headings and labels, a quiet grotesk for reading, every control a square cell that fills solid black when active, pierced-square rows as ornament, double-ruled cartouches around the work. Product screenshots are the only saturated color. Dark mode inverts to lacquer black with white rules.
STORY: the visitor sees a monogram and one line, "software que vai pro ar", understands that four real products are live, believes a senior engineer who runs his own products can run theirs, and writes the project to him.
FIRST VIEWPORT: nav as ruled square cells (monogram cell left, links, solid black "Contar projeto" cell right). Below it a headline in light caps over eight columns with the sub line and two cells (solid black CTA, outlined link). Beside it the solid Didone monogram, traced once by a pen line and then inked. The bottom of the viewport is cut by four square windows across the full width, each a peephole onto a live product, so the work is the first thing the scroll reveals.
FORM: design-canon-hoffmann-gridsmith-showroom (challenger, won over the roll's lambe-lambe poster wall after the user's logo showed a thin, geometric, minimalist taste); seed key 155a99ff.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
